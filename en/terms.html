The scope is defined as a list: which processes, which channels, which integrations, which scenarios. Anything not on the list is not part of the work — that is not a refusal, it is how we avoid two different readings of the word “turnkey”. New ideas mid-project are perfectly normal: we agree separately on what they do to the price and the timeline.

Tell me about anything suspicious as soon as you can: reacting together is faster.

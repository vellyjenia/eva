The client wanted

Shipment status lived in managers’ heads and in chats with six carriers. Money had to be reconciled by hand, and the owner answered the team’s routine questions herself. The company needed one assistant for everyone that knows its rules and sees real data.

We built

An AI agent in Telegram for the owner and managers, with different access rights. You can text it or send voice notes and photos. It connects to the CRM, email, company spreadsheets, call recordings and the company handbook. Alongside it runs a mini app with CRM data that you can edit.

How it works

For managers
  • Answers from the handbook: logistics, prices, procedures, scripts.
  • Helps with client replies, upsells and translation into Chinese for suppliers.
  • Reads chat screenshots, product photos and invoices.
  • Finds a client, their shipment and their supplier.
  • Checks a supplier on formal criteria and says what a person still has to verify.
Documents
  • Prepares contracts from fixed legal text, filling in only the client’s details.
  • Builds invoices and packing lists, and updates them without starting over.
  • Finds the right spot for a signature and stamp, makes PDFs and presentations.
Money
  • Calculates exchange rates and payment costs by company rules.
  • Watches the broker balance, margin, stuck requests and commission in managers’ reports.
  • Tracks expenses, prepares the monthly P&L and files for the accountant.
  • Code does the sums, not the model, so the numbers don’t drift.
Control
  • Sends the owner a digest every morning: money and actions needed.
  • Shows deals that need attention, manager rankings and quality scores.
  • Keeps a shipment register and reminds about delivery stages.
  • Reads calls and email, creates CRM deals, sends messages to the team.
Mini app
  • Deals, tasks, logistics and payments from the CRM, right in Telegram.
  • A manager updates the deal checklist, next actions and next contact time.
  • Moves deals along a kanban board, closes tasks, changes payment status and date.
  • Everyone sees and edits only their own items, and changes reach the CRM after confirmation.

Our estimate, November 2026.

The client wanted

The owner couldn’t read every manager’s client chats each day. She learned about a lost request or a weak reply too late, and motivation ran on impressions. She needed every conversation checked and a fair score.

We built

An AI agent that reads manager and client chats, scores them against company rules and reports in Telegram every day. The scores feed the motivation system.

How it works

Daily review
  • After the workday it reviews each chat: need, status, agreements, sales opportunity.
  • Backs every conclusion with a quote from the chat and its time.
  • Gives a score and a daily summary. Code catches banned phrases before the model runs.
Daytime response
  • Every half hour it finds clients left without a reply and reminds the manager.
Coaching
  • Remembers repeated mistakes and sets a focus for the day.
  • Shows the win of the day and suggests a phrase to try.
Motivation
  • Each week it counts leads, upsells, next steps and average order, and writes a profile of each manager.
  • The owner gets a strategic report on the team.
  • Scores appear in the mini app, and the manager of the month gets a bonus.

The client wanted

Run a Telegram channel regularly and in her own voice, without spending the day on topics, text and images. And no post goes out without the owner’s approval.

We built

An agent that finds news, builds a content plan in Google Sheets, writes the post, creates a cover and sends a preview for approval. After a yes, it publishes to the channel on schedule. The owner also tracks Instagram leads in it.

How it works

News and topics
  • Collects a news roundup twice a day, plus weekly references from selected accounts.
  • Researches one topic every day and proposes a post from it.
  • Forward a news item, and the agent rewrites it in your voice.
Content plan
  • The plan lives in Google Sheets: date, time, text, image, status.
  • The agent’s ideas land in the plan as normal rows.
  • Date and time can be changed with a button.
Text and images
  • Writes in the author’s voice, and a separate check filters AI tells and weak drafts.
  • Suggests three visual metaphors for a topic and generates the cover.
  • Rewrite the text or swap the image with one button.
Approval and publishing
  • Every post reaches the owner as a private preview.
  • Approval is tied to the exact text and image: if the post changes, it needs approval again.
  • Reminds in the morning about unanswered previews, and the daily model budget is capped.
Instagram leads
  • Shows leads in their own mini app section.
  • Uses diagnostic answers to spot the person’s main problem and drafts a personal message.
  • The owner opens the chat and sends it herself, the agent doesn’t message clients.

What it looks like
